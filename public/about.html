About Me

Hello, I'm Kamaldeen Sanusi, a Mechanical Engineer and Environmental Project Engineer with a passion for solving industrial challenges through sustainable engineering solutions.

Over the years, I have built hands-on experience in the design, assessment, commissioning, and optimization of Effluent Treatment Plants (ETPs) across multiple industries, including plastic recycling, food and beverage, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, manufacturing, and agro-processing. My work focuses on helping organizations meet environmental compliance requirements while improving operational efficiency and reducing their environmental footprint.

Currently, I work as a Project Engineer at an environmental consulting firm, where I am involved in the complete lifecycle of wastewater treatment projects—from site inspections and process design to equipment selection, commissioning, troubleshooting, and performance evaluation. I also contribute to workplace safety initiatives by supporting health, safety, and environmental (HSE) compliance.

My technical interests include:

  • Industrial wastewater treatment and recycling

  • Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) design and optimization

  • Water quality monitoring and laboratory analysis

  • Mechanical systems design

  • Environmental compliance and sustainability

  • Process improvement and industrial engineering

I enjoy transforming complex engineering challenges into practical, cost-effective solutions. Whether it is designing treatment systems, calculating equipment capacities, developing standard operating procedures, or improving existing plants, I believe engineering should create lasting value for both businesses and the environment.

Beyond engineering, I am committed to continuous learning. I actively expand my knowledge in areas such as data analysis, digital engineering tools, and emerging technologies that can improve engineering decision-making and project delivery. I believe that combining traditional engineering principles with modern technology is essential for building smarter and more sustainable industries.

My long-term vision is to become a recognized engineering professional who contributes to cleaner industries, stronger environmental stewardship, and innovative infrastructure across Africa.

When I'm not working on engineering projects, I enjoy researching new technologies, learning new technical skills, mentoring others where I can, and exploring innovative solutions to real-world industrial problems.

Thank you for visiting my website. I look forward to connecting with professionals, organizations, and partners who share a commitment to engineering excellence, sustainability, and innovation.
