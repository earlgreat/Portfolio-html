Kamal Best Movies

My top five movies of All time

The Pursuit of Happyness

Chris Gardner struggles with financial hardship while raising his son. Despite homelessness and countless setbacks, he pursues a stockbroker internship and eventually succeeds through perseverance and hard work.

3 Idiots

Three engineering students navigate the pressures of university education. The film challenges conventional ideas of success and encourages pursuing passion and practical learning.

The Shawshank Redemption

A banker wrongfully imprisoned spends years maintaining hope and planning for freedom. It is a powerful story about resilience, patience, and determination.

The Social Network

Follows Mark Zuckerberg's journey from Harvard student to co-founder of Facebook, highlighting innovation, ambition, business growth, and the personal conflicts that arose along the way.

Akeelah and the Bee

A young girl from a disadvantaged background competes in the National Spelling Bee. The film emphasizes discipline, education, mentorship, and perseverance.
